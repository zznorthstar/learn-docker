# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback graciously, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report concerns privately through a [GitHub security advisory contact](https://github.com/zznorthstar/learn-docker/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer, Hamdi, via the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially and the maintainer may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.
